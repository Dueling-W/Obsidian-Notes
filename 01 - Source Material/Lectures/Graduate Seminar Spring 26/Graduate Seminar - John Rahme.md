## Notes

- Focus is on reliability modeling of systems
	- Like failure mechanisms in traditional software
		- Resource age, workload stress, repair/restart state
- With AI: "keep the system trustworthy while it runs"
	- New concerns are  wrong prediction while the service stays up
	- model drfit/performance deacy
	- halluicnation/confident wrong outputs
	- etc.
- Research moving from layer 1 (no AI, focusing on software aging/failure modeling) to layer 2 (AI as a subsystem, how does adding AI change things) to layer 3 (AI-specific degredaration like drift, modeling aging, and hallucnations)
- Cloud AI is concerned with uptime and availability 
- Mission-critical AI systems
	- Reliability must be evaluated together with safety margin and human supervisory control
- With  the new AI systems, there are new reliability metrics to add (prediction error over time, unsafe action rate, halluicnation rate, etc.)

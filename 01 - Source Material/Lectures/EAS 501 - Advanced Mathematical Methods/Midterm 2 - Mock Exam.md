# Midterm 2 Mock Exam — EAS 501

**Choose four problems among the six.** 1 hr 15 min. Calculator and formula sheet allowed. Each problem is worth 25 points.

---

**1.** Let
$$\mathbf v_1 = \begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}, \quad \mathbf v_2 = \begin{pmatrix}2\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}, \quad \mathbf v_3 = \begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}$$
(a) Apply the Gram–Schmidt process to obtain an orthonormal basis $\{\hat{\mathbf e}_1, \hat{\mathbf e}_2, \hat{\mathbf e}_3\}$.

(b) Find the best approximation of $\mathbf u = (3,1,2)^T$ in $\text{span}\{\hat{\mathbf e}_1, \hat{\mathbf e}_2\}$, and compute the error $|\mathbf E|$.

---

**2.** Let
$$A = \begin{pmatrix}1&-1&2&0&1\\2&-2&5&1&3\\1&-1&3&1&2\end{pmatrix}$$
(a) Find a basis for the null space of $A$, and find the rank of $A$.

(b) For which value(s) of $k$ is $A\mathbf x = (1,3,k)^T$ consistent? When it is consistent, how many free parameters does the solution have? Give one particular solution.

(c) Are the columns of $A$ linearly independent? Justify your answer.

---

**3.** For
$$A = \begin{pmatrix}1&2&-1\\2&1&1\\-1&4&2\end{pmatrix}$$
find the $LU$ factorization of $A$, with $l_{ii} = 1$. Then use it to solve $A\mathbf x = (-3,3,-1)^T$.

---

**4.** Let
$$A = \begin{pmatrix}2&1&0\\1&1&1\\0&1&3\end{pmatrix}$$
(a) Use elementary row operations to obtain $A^{-1}$.

(b) Use $A^{-1}$ to solve $A\mathbf x = (1,2,3)^T$.

(c) Verify $x_2$ using Cramer's rule.

---

**5.** Let
$$A = \begin{pmatrix}4&0&1\\-2&1&0\\-2&0&1\end{pmatrix}$$
(a) Find the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of $A$.

(b) Find $Q$ and a diagonal $D$ with $Q^{-1}AQ = D$.

(c) Can $A$ be diagonalized in the form $Q^TAQ = D$ with $Q$ orthogonal? Explain.

---

**6.** For the symmetric matrix
$$A = \begin{pmatrix}2&-1&-1\\-1&2&-1\\-1&-1&2\end{pmatrix}$$
(a) Diagonalize $A$ in the form $Q^TAQ = D$, with $D$ diagonal.

(b) Use (a) to reduce the quadratic form $f = 2x_1^2 + 2x_2^2 + 2x_3^2 - 2x_1x_2 - 2x_1x_3 - 2x_2x_3$ to canonical form. Is $f$ positive definite?

## Notes
- Number crunching --> productivity/interactive --> streaming information to/from physical world
	- Fueled by extremely small microprocessors
- Moore's Law --> number of transistors on microchips doubles every two years
	- Notable: 2 nm (nano-meter) size transistor in 2025
	- Not just transistors --> memory, processors, and disk space
- Single processors --> ILP (instruction level parallelism)
- Multiprocessors use:
	- Thread level parallelism (TLP)
	- Data level parallelism (DLP) 
		- Same operation on different blocks of data
		- AI benefit --> can use smaller data blocks if large blocks (doubles) are wasteful
- Amdahl's law --> compute/speedup from # of processors limited by parallel portion of code (or, inversely, serial portion)
	- Even 95% parallel eventually goes to a limit
- General design principles
	- Need to parallelize things (do this at the hardware level)
	- Locality --> how we have different levels of cache
